Tips for best results
  • Export PDFs with text as outlines for print, but know that OCR accuracy on outlined text is lower. The tool renders each page at 200 DPI and applies OCR — results are best-effort.
  • Upload one PDF per SKU for the clearest per-SKU report.
  • The GTIN cross-check only works if you upload the master GTIN/SKU spreadsheet.
  • Eyemark analysis uses the bottom-right corner of the rendered image. If your die line puts the eyemark elsewhere, verify that check manually.
  • FDA flags are pattern-matched from OCR text — they are a starting point, not legal advice. Always have a regulatory professional review final labels.
