Flex Ordering Logic

When demand falls between multiples of an MOQ, you have two options:

  1. Round up to the next full MOQ multiple
  2. Mix products using the remaining super sack capacity
    e.g. 700 pouches needed → order 500 pouches + ~6,857 stick packs to fill the remainder of the sack

The calculator above shows both options when applicable.
